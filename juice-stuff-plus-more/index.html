About

Meet The Juice Plug

Juice "STUFF" + More, LLC believes wellness should feel good, taste good, and look good. We're not just a juice brand — we're a lifestyle movement built on health, hustle, and real community. Our mission is simple: to keep you charged, balanced, and feeling your absolute best, every single day.

Founded by Lasharn D. Hamilton, aka The Juice Plug, our brand was born out of a personal journey of healing and transformation. Lasharn is a Certified Juice Therapist and Holistic Health Practitioner. What started as a private quest for better energy and healthier habits became a passion for sharing fresh, natural fuel with the people around us. The more we pressed, blended, and created, the more it became clear:

Real ingredients + real intention = real results.
